What's inside

  • Game design — four titles. Dream and 0423 (first-person horror, solo, UE5), Jim's Adventure (3D action roguelike, solo), and Clay Beats, a couch co-op rhythm game released on Steam where I owned animation production and core interaction design.
  • Systems design in production — a character presentation state machine I solely owned, a wardrobe and customisation system, gameplay-mode logic and boss phases, and an AI-assisted pipeline that took one mode's configured roster from 20 to 110+ behind a validation gate. Described at architecture level only; this work is covered by an NDA.
  • AI-assisted art pipelines — concept to rigged, engine-ready character; a method for generating orthographic views that actually agree; image-to-3D through retopology, PBR and skinning.
  • Tools and full-stack work — a prediction platform that ran a full season for 106 users behind a 70-test CI deploy gate, and a self-organising knowledge system built as a graph.
  • Original visual work — an image series about the interiors of a 1990s–2000s Chinese childhood.
On generative AI: its use is disclosed in full on the final page of the portfolio, section by section. The game projects use none. Where generative tools appear, they are the explicit subject of the work, and the direction, correction, rigging and in-engine results are mine.
